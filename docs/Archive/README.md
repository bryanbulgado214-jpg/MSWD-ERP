# Archive

Superseded documents, kept for historical reference — not current
guidance.

## What belongs here

- Prior revisions of documents once a newer revision is approved (e.g. a
  "Database Architecture — Revision 1" document, once Revision 2 is
  approved and becomes the current version in `04_Database/`).
- Old proposals or designs that were considered and explicitly not
  pursued, kept so the reasoning isn't lost and doesn't get re-litigated
  from scratch later.

## What doesn't belong here

- Superseded ADRs — those stay in `10_Decisions/` permanently; a new ADR
  supersedes an old one in place, it doesn't get archived (see that
  folder's own README).
- Anything still actively referenced or followed — if it's current
  guidance, it belongs in its proper numbered folder, not here.

## Convention

When archiving a document, keep its original filename and prefix it with
the date it was superseded, e.g.
`2026-07-28_database-architecture-revision-1.md`, so it's clear at a
glance both what it was and when it stopped being current.
